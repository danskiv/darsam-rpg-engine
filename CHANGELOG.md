# Darsam RPG Engine - Changelog

## [2.4.1-dev] - 2026-08-22 (Branch: feat/encounter-ecosystem)
### Added
- **Expanded Cross-Class Secret Awakening Tree:** Added multiple diverse jobs across all tiers starting from Common to Mythic (Common: Sellsword, Apothecary, Scavenger; Special: Pyromancer, Juggernaut; Epic: Storm-Weaver, Plague-Lord; Legendary: Solar Archangel, Ashen Dread-Emperor; Mythic: Primordial Ouroboros).
- **Freeform Cross-Classing:** Players can fully cross over disciplines (e.g. Peasant into Pyromancer, or Scholar into Dread-Emperor) upon encountering mysterious dungeon events.

## [2.4.0-dev] - 2026-08-22 (Branch: feat/encounter-ecosystem)
### Added
- **Dynamic Living Crypt Merchant & Barter Encampment UI:** Full interactive merchant shop embedded in controller when entering `merchant` node type.
- **Haggling via Charisma Check (`CHA Check vs DC 11`):** Roll D20 to attempt haggling prices with merchants.
- **One-Click "Sell All Junk" Engine:** Instant liquidation of all common white/gray gear & materials in 40-slot backpack.
- **Weighted Encounter Generator Architecture:** 6-tier probabilistic room encounter generator.
