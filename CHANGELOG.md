# Darsam RPG Engine - Changelog

## [2.3.3] - 2026-08-22
### Added
- **Deterministic Milestone-Based Chapter & Floor Progression:** Structured chapter advancement (Chapter 1–5 across Floors 1–3) based on exploration step milestones, ending infinite loops on Chapter 1.
